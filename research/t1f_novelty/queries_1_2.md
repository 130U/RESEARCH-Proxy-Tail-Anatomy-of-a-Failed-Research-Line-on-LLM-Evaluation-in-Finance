## Where the results went and follow-up tracing

- Batches 0–1 located Gittings, XBRL and the background on rounding in filings; batches 2–3 checked QA, ACL, arXiv and benchmark-community keywords. Queries combining FinQA/TAT-QA/ConvFinQA with input precision/condition number did not return any full text that demonstrably completes candidate 1; this is only the current search result.
- Batches 4–8 pursued accounting relations, financial ratios, interval arithmetic and rounded data. The XBRL specification is a clear positive hit; for the Chinese "automatic adjustment of statements" lead only abstracts/reprinted fragments were obtained, which cannot be promoted to a precedent for exact recovery.
- Batches 9–10 searched work on reconstruction in statistical disclosure and obtained the full text of Dandekar 2010; following its numerical-optimisation theme, the journal full text of Salazar-González 2005 was read.
- Batches 9–11 looked for the source of Higham's principle and reached the author's 2021 original; for the 2002 textbook only the bibliographic information was verified, and there is no claim that the book was read in full.
- Batches 12–13 found VeriFin, after which the mathematical definition in v3 §3.6 was read directly to distinguish input point values from output tolerance.
- The specified queries in native GitHub GH1 (Arelle) and GH2 (EnvCommons) returned empty; the whole-repository query in GH2 returned 10 items, most of them irrelevant to this verdict, and they are not counted as relevant papers. Another QA-grader lead is [whileai-sdk #255](https://github.com/LMikeH/whileai-sdk/issues/255), which self-reports improving grader false negatives on an SEC XBRL task, but it is not an analysis of FinQA input rounding.
- The root agent separately and independently read EnvCommons PR #2/#3/#4; the conclusions are included in the overall report, and the empty issues result from this subgroup cannot override the direct PR evidence.
- Yan 2026 (arXiv 2611.02461) was passed over from the candidate 3 subgroup; its full text §§1–6 and Appendix D/E were read directly. The location of the FinanceReasoning appendix was passed over from the candidate 4/5 subgroup, and this subgroup re-read the relevant HTML. These are **not fabricated as additional keyword searches**.
- The citation chains mainly follow the related work and references of XFinBench, FinanceReasoning and RAG-CoT into later evaluations of FinQA/TAT-QA/ConvFinQA, the XBRL specifications and statistical disclosure methods. No machine export of all papers citing the three benchmarks was obtained, and this local tracing is not described as a complete traversal of the citation graph.
