## Screening rules for this round

Direct match: attribution/proportion of FinQA rejections, per-question cross-model comparison of wrong questions on original or derived FinQA, annotation revisions of financial numeric benchmarks and their effect on scores. General background: label audits of other benchmarks. Excluded: pure model-capability leaderboards, second-hand summaries without the original, mistaking the MultiHiertt proportion for FinQA, mistaking code-rewrite validation for field-conflict statistics. Full texts that could not be accessed are kept as gaps and cannot be used as definite counter-evidence.
